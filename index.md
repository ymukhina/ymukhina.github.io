## About me

I am a postdoc in the Applied Algebra and Geometry research group at the [Department of Mathematical Sciences](https://www.math.ku.dk), University of Copenhagen.

I completed my PhD in October 2026 in the [MAX team](https://www.lix.polytechnique.fr/max/max-web/max/max-home.en.html) of the [Laboratoire d’informatique de l’École polytechnique](https://www.lix.polytechnique.fr), Institut Polytechnique de Paris, under the supervision of [Gleb Pogudin](https://www.lix.polytechnique.fr/Labo/Gleb.POGUDIN/) and [Joris van der Hoeven](https://www.texmacs.org/joris/main/joris.html).
